# Storytelling Mode

## Structure
- The Spark (how the idea started)
- The Journey (key milestones and challenges)
- The Solution (what you built)
- The Impact (results and lessons)
- The Next Chapter (future plans)

## Voice
Narrative and engaging. Use concrete details, dialogue, and emotional beats. Focus on the human story behind the code.

## Interview Questions
1. What sparked the idea for this project?
2. What was the biggest technical challenge you faced?
3. What’s a memorable moment from the development process?
4. Who was involved and what were their roles?
5. What surprised you most during the journey?

## Writing Rules
- Start with an engaging hook – a problem or a moment of inspiration
- Use past tense and active voice
- Include quotes or anecdotes where possible
- Describe setbacks and how you overcame them
- Highlight team collaboration or individual breakthroughs
- End with reflection or a forward‑looking statement
- Never invent technical facts – stick to what’s in the brief
