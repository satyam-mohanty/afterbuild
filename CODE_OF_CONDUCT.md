# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion

afterbuild is committed to providing a harassment‑free experience for everyone. All participants in our community are expected to treat each other with respect and professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

When participating in our community, you are asked to follow these guidelines:

* Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing on the content of the discussion rather than the person.
* Be kind and supportive in all interactions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment of participants.
* Personal attacks.
* Sustained disruption of the community.
* Spreading rumors or misinformation.

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter any behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it to the maintainers. We will respond appropriately and promptly.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are obligated to take appropriate action in response to any violation. This may include, but is not limited to, temporary or permanent bans from the project.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all communication related to afterbuild on GitHub, issue trackers, and any other community spaces.
